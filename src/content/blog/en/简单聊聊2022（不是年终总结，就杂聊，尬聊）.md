Tomorrow is the 31st, the last day of 2021. I have a feeling 2022 will be an extraordinary year—the year I make the most progress yet. I have so many plans: get my driver's license, improve my English, learn Rust, contribute to more open-source projects, and read more books. I need to put in a little more effort than usual. I also want to spend some time recording videos for Bilibili. I have started experimenting with explaining topics and concepts in a format other than articles, and I plan to record my presentations at work, along with the things I have been thinking about and learning.

Why make videos? Mainly to practice speaking. Trying to communicate with an audience helps me understand a subject more deeply, and makes what I learn stick longer than writing an article does. That is the ultimate goal.

People in Xi'an have really been having a rough time lately, myself included (honestly, I could cry; I have no words). I cannot say too much about Xi'an in a public post, but dealing with all this just before Chinese New Year is incredibly upsetting. When the outbreak began, I joined Xu's small team to plan an app for finding COVID testing sites. It went viral just one day after launch. Our teammates were interviewed by quite a few TV stations and newspapers, and we even made Weibo's trending list. We had our moment in the spotlight and did something meaningful along the way.

Coming up:

1. The Pinia persistence plugin I wrote two or three months ago: I have really wanted to release it, but other things keep getting in the way. I hope to finish it as soon as possible in January.
2. A Vue 3 source-code walkthrough series (I have written five or six posts; getting them published before Chinese New Year would already be an achievement).
3. An article organizing my notes on Rust ownership (two paragraphs written so far).
4. Frontend architecture workflow design (essentially a set of reading notes).
