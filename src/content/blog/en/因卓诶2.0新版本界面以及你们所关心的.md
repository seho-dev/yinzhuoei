**Before We Begin**

The blog has been online for a few months, and both its search engine visibility and the feedback have been good. Lately, I haven't paid much attention to content syndication—these are articles republished from WeChat Official Accounts, with permission. Version 2.0 will include less syndicated content and focus as much as possible on the best original writing, raising the standard of the technical blog.

**Why Build 2.0?**

We decided against a migration and discarded all the old data. There were too many things I regretted about 1.0; I hadn't really given it the care it deserved. I owe everyone an apology for that.

**Getting Ready for 2.0**

I settled on the new blogging system after a long conversation with someone online. The one requirement was not to use WordPress. We considered several alternatives and ultimately chose Typecho, an excellent blogging system developed in China. I fell in love with it the first time I used it—no flashy admin panel or frontend, just articles presented directly to readers, with a dramatic improvement in loading speed.

**Will the Content Change in 2.0?**

Syndication will continue, but unlike 1.0, we won't restrict ourselves to content from other IT news sites. We've selected better WeChat Official Accounts and international communities to keep the quality high. Sharing resources is what Yinzhuoei is all about, and 2.0 will carry that idea forward, not abandon it. We will also move away from basic programming material and gradually explore topics in greater depth.

**Goodbye, 1.0**
**Hello, 2.0**

It's me, Lao Shen.
