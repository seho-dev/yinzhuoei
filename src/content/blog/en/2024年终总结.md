## Introduction

**Location**: the dining table at my family home in Ankang, Shaanxi  
**Device**: an M1 Air I've been using for five years (oh fuck, this thing really lasts!)

Long time no see, everyone! Personal circumstances kept me from updating the blog for the last year or two, and I owe you an apology. It's the first day of the Lunar New Year, so I'm taking the opportunity to finally write my 2024 year-end review. I even forgot to write one for 2023, but that's all right.

Over these two years, I found the love of my life and am about to get married, and my career has long since settled into a steady rhythm. Technically, though—especially when it comes to frontend development—I've lost the drive to explore. I feel the entire frontend industry has entered a period of “stagnation,” so I sincerely hope people consider changing fields sooner rather than later. I wouldn't recommend frontend development to newcomers, either.

## Games

As a kid, I loved a game called **QQ Speed**, so I returned to it in 2024. I also frequently play two-player games with Little P, often well past midnight. If I'd known how much we'd enjoy them, I would have bought a Windows computer sooner!

To get more free items in QQ Speed, I even wrote an automatic check-in script. Using an **AutoIt script + a Xiaomi smart plug**, I had the computer run QQ Speed tasks automatically in the middle of the night to earn rare items. The lengths I'll go to for games! Yet when I finally got the racing car I'd dreamed of as a child, the excitement wasn't nearly as strong as I'd expected. Gradually, it started to feel rather hollow. (I still can't bring myself to spend money on an S-class car. At **RMB 2,000**, it goes completely against my spending habits to dump that much money into a game in one go. That's just crazy!)

## Work and Learning

For the last year or two, my work has still been in **frontend development (React)**. What can I say? I have to make a living. Over that time, though, I've thought more deeply about **large React projects**, and I pay closer attention to architecture and performance optimization when writing code.

In my spare time, I sometimes build **whatever feature comes to mind**, but those projects usually get shelved for lack of time. One example is **YourPanel**, which I open-sourced a while ago and still intend to continue. For now, though, it's on hold because my **Rust skills are limited**.

Recently, I've been helping an old friend by **working as a PM for free**. After my day job, I'm at it until 11 p.m., organizing requirements and assigning tasks to developers. Honestly, it's exhausting. Being a PM is no easier than being a programmer, but there is a lot to learn. I took it on because I wanted to address my weaknesses in **business logic** and get better at **connecting the pieces of a business workflow**. Even after all this time as a PM, though, I'm not sure I've fully solved that problem. Let's see how things go in 2025. If it helps, doing it for free will have been worth it—just tiring…

This year, I spent more time learning Rust than in previous years. My practice project was **Rustlings**, which is very beginner-friendly. It mainly consists of **test cases** where you fix **Rust compilation errors**, with each error grouped into a different chapter. If you get stuck, there are explanations to help you fix the code. Finishing an exercise gives you a real sense of accomplishment.

I **highly recommend** learning Rust, whether you're new to programming or an experienced frontend engineer! Although I've been learning it for **“several years”**, and have even started over **“many times,”** I still wouldn't say I've really got the basics down.

## Health

Please **take good care of yourselves**! I'm still young, but I've already developed some heart issues. I occasionally get palpitations, though they're infrequent. I never work overtime, either, so perhaps they're brought on by things that worry me sometimes.

I've had quite a few health problems over the last two years. Here are a few examples you might want to watch out for:

- **Heart palpitations** (if you have similar problems, you could try taking a trip to unwind; this can be hard to treat with medication. Monitoring your heart also requires wearing a device, which makes occasional episodes harder to catch.)
- **Sciatica** (pain in the buttocks and thighs from sitting too long. Moving around more can help; mine hasn't come back.)
- **Skin problems** (avoid harsh acid-based products in winter, and pay attention to hydration and sun protection in summer.)
