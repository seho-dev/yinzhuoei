## This Isn't a Sponsored Post—I'm Mostly Here to Share Beautiful Photos, Hehe

Location: Jinsi Gorge in Shangluo, Shaanxi
Admission: RMB 100 per adult
Overall impression: mountains and a gorge—the perfect combination. Hike the mountain first, then cool off with a walk through the gorge. Well worth the price.
Things to know: make sure you hike the mountain when you visit. If you take the gorge route first, you won't be able to do the mountain hike, but you can walk through the gorge after coming down.

## One Sad Exception

On the morning of the day I went up the mountain, I received the news that my maternal grandfather had passed away. After months of suffering, he had gone peacefully. The next day, I hurried back to my hometown for his funeral. This was my first real experience of taking part in a funeral. I vaguely remember attending my grandmother's when I was little, but I was so young that my mother carried me off to bed, and I didn't take part in the local incense-circling ritual. This time, I joined the prayers for my grandfather over several nights. I also got to see him the day before the burial, which was something I had hoped for. May he be happy in heaven, may he always have good liquor to drink, and may he always keep that childlike spirit.
